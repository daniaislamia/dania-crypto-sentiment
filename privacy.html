# Privacy Policy Dania Crypto Sentiment Analyzer is an educational project developed for university coursework. The application processes publicly available cryptocurrency-related posts on X to classify sentiment. The data may include post text, post identifiers, timestamps, cryptocurrency symbols, and sentiment labels. The processed data may be stored in Google Sheets for historical sentiment analysis. Summary reports may be sent to a private Telegram chat. The application does not intentionally collect users' private messages, passwords, or personal contact information. Data is handled for educational analysis and should be retained only as permitted by applicable X policies. For questions about this project, contact the project owner.
